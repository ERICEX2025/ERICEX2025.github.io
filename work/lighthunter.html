Lighthunter

May 2026 – Present Technical Cofounder Film & Production

I'm the technical cofounder and sole engineer of Lighthunter, building the whole product alongside an industry cofounder: a web app (Next.js / Supabase), native iPhone and iPad apps (SwiftUI), and a Chrome extension, all sharing one real-time backend and an AI layer.

Next.js Supabase SwiftUI TypeScript Claude Chrome Extension Semantic Search

One Synced Product, Three Surfaces

Solo-built one live, synced product across three surfaces: a web app (Next.js / Supabase), native iPhone and iPad apps (SwiftUI), and a Chrome extension — all sharing the same real-time data.

Physics-Based Lighting Designer

Built a physics-based lighting designer that lets cinematographers plan and visualize lighting setups before they ever step on set.

Claude AI Layer

Layered Claude throughout the product: semantic shot-list search, look-based reference search, an auto-tagged searchable library, and call-sheet / shot-list parsing.

Reflection

Lighthunter is the project I keep coming back to. It started from a simple idea: cinematographers deserve better tools for planning their work, and I wanted to be the one to build them. I'm the whole engineering team across web, native iOS, a Chrome extension, and an AI layer, so every product and technical decision is mine to make. It's a long-term build alongside an industry cofounder, and it's where I get to push my range as a product engineer: shipping real features for real users, and obsessing over the UX until the tools disappear into their workflow on set.
